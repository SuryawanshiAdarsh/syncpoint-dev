## About / Description (2,000 char limit)

```
Syncpoint helps startups doing their first SOC 2 audit collect, organize, and
map compliance evidence — without pretending an algorithm can tell you you're
compliant.

Most compliance-automation tools sell a black-box "trust score" and ask you to
take it on faith. Syncpoint doesn't. It connects to the systems you already use,
lets AI propose where a piece of evidence maps to a control, and requires a
human on your team to confirm every single mapping before it counts. Every
suggestion and every confirmation is permanently logged — so your auditor sees
exactly what happened, not a vendor's percentage.

Built for engineering-led teams doing their first SOC 2 audit who want
something they, and their auditor, can actually verify.

Runs as a normal SaaS app — or as a single container entirely inside your own
infrastructure, if you'd rather your cloud credentials never leave your network.
```
