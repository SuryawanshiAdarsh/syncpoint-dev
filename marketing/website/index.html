The problem

Traditional SOC 2 prep takes a quarter, not a sprint.

Security leads spend months hunting screenshots over Slack, dumping them into a shared drive, and manually mapping each one to a control in a spreadsheet — only to find half the evidence has gone stale by the time the audit actually starts.

What Syncpoint actually does

Everything a real SOC 2 program needs

Not a single evidence inbox — the full program, end to end.

rocket_launch

Guided SOC 2 kickoff

Trust Services Category scoping, Type I vs Type II selection with observation-period tracking, and a structured system-description builder — done in a guided flow, not a blank onboarding tour.

verified_user

Risk register (CC3)

Score risks by likelihood × impact, assign owners, link them to the controls that mitigate them, and track remediation status — the risk assessment every SOC 2 report requires.

bolt

Continuous evidence automation

Deterministic collectors for GitHub, AWS, and Jira pull real evidence on a schedule. No AI guessing at facts an API can just tell us.

psychology

AI-assisted mapping, human-confirmed

AI proposes which control a piece of evidence supports, with cited reasoning. A human reviewer always makes the final call — every suggestion and confirmation is permanently logged.

gavel

Policy management

Upload, version, and track staff acknowledgment of every required policy, mapped directly to the controls they support.

fact_check

Auditor-ready readiness report

One document: coverage percentage, open gaps, the full risk register, and any logged control exceptions — the artifact you hand your CPA firm to start scoping.

verified

Built on honesty, not a black-box score.

Every AI suggestion is labeled AI_SUGGESTED until a human confirms it. Nothing in your audit trail pretends a human didn't make the final call — that's the difference between a real audit trail and a vendor's opaque "trust score."
