# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Starting template.** Customize this document to reflect your organization's actual
> practices before adopting it as policy. This is not legal advice.

## Purpose
Defines acceptable and unacceptable use of company systems, devices, accounts, and data by
employees and contractors.

## Scope
Applies to all company-owned or company-managed devices, accounts, networks, and data, and to
personal devices used to access them.

## Policy

1. **Authorized use.** Company systems and accounts are to be used primarily for legitimate
   business purposes. Incidental, reasonable personal use is permitted unless it interferes
   with work or violates other policies.
2. **Prohibited activity.** Users must not use company systems to engage in illegal activity,
   harassment, unauthorized data access, or circumvention of security controls.
3. **Device security.** Devices used to access company systems must have disk encryption,
   an up-to-date operating system, and endpoint protection where applicable, and must be
   locked when unattended.
4. **Software installation.** Only approved software may be installed on company-managed
   devices; unauthorized software must not be installed without IT approval.
5. **Data handling.** Company and customer data must not be stored on unapproved personal
   devices, cloud accounts, or removable media.
6. **Credentials.** Users must not share their credentials or leave sessions unattended and
   authenticated on shared devices.
7. **Reporting.** Users must report lost/stolen devices and suspected policy violations
   immediately.

## Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination
of employment or contract.
