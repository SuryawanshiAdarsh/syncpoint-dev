# Access Control Policy

> **Starting template.** Customize this document to reflect your organization's actual
> practices before adopting it as policy. This is not legal advice.

## Purpose
Defines how access to systems, applications, and data is requested, approved, granted,
reviewed, and revoked.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and automated/service accounts with access to company
systems.

## Policy

1. **Least privilege.** Access is granted only to the systems and data required for a
   person's role.
2. **Provisioning.** New access requests require manager or system-owner approval before
   being granted.
3. **Authentication.** All systems that support it must enforce multi-factor authentication
   (MFA) for interactive logins.
4. **Unique identity.** Shared accounts are prohibited except where technically unavoidable,
   and any exception must be documented and compensating-controlled.
5. **Periodic access review.** Access rights are reviewed at least quarterly by system
   owners; unnecessary access is revoked promptly.
6. **Offboarding.** Access is revoked within one business day of termination or role change
   that no longer requires it.
7. **Privileged access.** Administrative/privileged accounts are limited to personnel whose
   role requires it, logged, and reviewed more frequently than standard access (e.g. monthly).
8. **Password requirements.** Where MFA is not available, passwords must meet a minimum
   complexity/length standard defined in the Password & Cryptography Policy.

## Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination
of employment or contract.
