# Asset Management Policy

> **Starting template.** Customize this document to reflect your organization's actual
> practices before adopting it as policy. This is not legal advice.

## Purpose
Defines how the organization inventories, classifies, and manages the lifecycle of hardware
and software assets that store or process company or customer data.

## Scope
Applies to all company-owned devices (laptops, servers, mobile devices), cloud infrastructure,
and licensed software.

## Policy

1. **Asset inventory.** An inventory of hardware assets and critical cloud infrastructure
   (e.g. servers, databases, storage) is maintained and kept current.
2. **Ownership.** Each asset has a designated owner responsible for its security and
   appropriate use.
3. **Classification.** Assets are classified based on the sensitivity of data they store or
   process, aligned with the Information Security Policy's data classification scheme.
4. **Secure configuration.** Assets are configured according to a documented baseline
   (e.g. disk encryption, endpoint protection, patching) before being placed into use.
5. **Patch management.** Operating systems and critical software are kept up to date with
   security patches on a defined cadence, prioritized by severity.
6. **Decommissioning.** When an asset is retired, data is securely wiped or the media is
   destroyed before disposal or reassignment, per the Data Retention & Disposal Policy.
7. **Lost/stolen assets.** Loss or theft of a company asset must be reported immediately so
   access can be revoked and the device remotely wiped where possible.

## Enforcement
Failure to report a lost or stolen asset promptly is a policy violation.
