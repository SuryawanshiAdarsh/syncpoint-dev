# Vendor & Third-Party Risk Management Policy

> **Starting template.** Customize this document to reflect your organization's actual
> practices before adopting it as policy. This is not legal advice.

## Purpose
Establishes how the organization evaluates and manages security risk introduced by vendors,
subprocessors, and other third parties.

## Scope
Applies to any third party that stores, processes, or has access to company or customer data,
or that provides a service material to the organization's operations.

## Policy

1. **Risk tiering.** Vendors are tiered by the sensitivity of data/access they receive and
   the criticality of the service they provide.
2. **Due diligence.** Before onboarding, higher-tier vendors are assessed for security posture
   (e.g. security questionnaire, SOC 2 report review, or equivalent).
3. **Contractual protections.** Agreements with vendors handling confidential or customer data
   include appropriate data protection and confidentiality terms.
4. **Ongoing monitoring.** Critical vendors are reassessed periodically (e.g. annually) or
   upon material change in the relationship.
5. **Subprocessor list.** A current list of subprocessors that handle customer data is
   maintained and made available to customers where contractually required.
6. **Offboarding.** Access granted to a vendor is revoked promptly when the relationship ends.
7. **Incident notification.** Vendor contracts require timely notification of security
   incidents affecting company or customer data.

## Enforcement
Onboarding a vendor without required due diligence is a policy violation.
