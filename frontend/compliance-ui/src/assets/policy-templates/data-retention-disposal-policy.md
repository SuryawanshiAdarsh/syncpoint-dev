# Data Retention & Disposal Policy

> **Starting template.** Customize this document to reflect your organization's actual
> practices before adopting it as policy. This is not legal advice.

## Purpose
Defines how long different categories of data are retained and how they are securely disposed
of when no longer needed.

## Scope
Applies to all company and customer data, in any format, held by the organization or its
vendors.

## Policy

1. **Retention schedule.** Each major data category (e.g. customer data, employee records,
   logs, backups, financial records) has a defined minimum and maximum retention period based
   on business need and legal/regulatory requirements.
2. **Data minimization.** Data is not retained longer than necessary to fulfill its original
   purpose or a legal obligation.
3. **Secure disposal.** Data past its retention period is securely deleted or destroyed such
   that it cannot be reconstructed (e.g. cryptographic erasure, secure wipe, physical
   destruction of media).
4. **Customer data on termination.** Upon contract termination, customer data is deleted or
   returned within a defined period per the customer agreement.
5. **Backups.** Backup retention periods are defined and backups are included in the disposal
   process once their retention window expires.
6. **Legal hold.** Data subject to a legal hold or active investigation is exempted from
   routine disposal until the hold is lifted.

## Enforcement
Retaining or disposing of data outside this policy without an approved exception is a policy
violation.
