# Incident Response Policy

> **Starting template.** Customize this document to reflect your organization's actual
> practices before adopting it as policy. This is not legal advice.

## Purpose
Establishes how the organization detects, responds to, contains, and recovers from security
incidents, and how it communicates about them.

## Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and systems capable of generating or being affected by
a security incident.

## Policy

1. **Reporting.** Any employee who suspects a security incident must report it immediately to
   the designated incident response contact/channel.
2. **Classification.** Incidents are triaged and classified by severity (e.g. Low, Medium,
   High, Critical) based on scope and impact.
3. **Response team.** A designated incident response lead coordinates investigation,
   containment, eradication, and recovery for each incident.
4. **Containment & eradication.** Affected systems are isolated as needed to prevent further
   impact while preserving evidence for investigation.
5. **Communication.** Customers, regulators, and other stakeholders are notified in
   accordance with contractual and legal obligations and applicable breach-notification laws.
6. **Post-incident review.** A post-mortem is conducted for all Medium+ severity incidents,
   documenting root cause, timeline, and corrective actions.
7. **Testing.** The incident response plan is tested (e.g. via tabletop exercise) at least
   annually.

## Enforcement
Failure to report a known or suspected incident promptly may result in disciplinary action.
