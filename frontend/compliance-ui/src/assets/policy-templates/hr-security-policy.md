# HR Security Policy (Onboarding & Offboarding)

> **Starting template.** Customize this document to reflect your organization's actual
> practices before adopting it as policy. This is not legal advice.

## Purpose
Defines the security-relevant steps required when employees and contractors join, change
roles within, or leave the organization.

## Scope
Applies to all employees and contractors.

## Policy

1. **Background checks.** Background checks are performed for new hires where legally
   permitted and appropriate to the role, prior to granting access to sensitive systems.
2. **Confidentiality agreements.** All new hires and contractors sign a confidentiality/
   non-disclosure agreement before receiving system access.
3. **Security training.** New hires complete security awareness training as part of
   onboarding, before or immediately after receiving system access.
4. **Access provisioning.** System access is provisioned based on role, following the
   least-privilege principle defined in the Access Control Policy.
5. **Role changes.** When an employee changes roles, access is reviewed and adjusted to match
   the new role's requirements; access no longer needed is revoked.
6. **Offboarding.** Upon termination, all system access, badges, and company-owned devices are
   revoked/returned promptly (target: within one business day), and the departure is
   communicated to relevant teams.
7. **Exit interview.** Departing employees with access to sensitive systems are reminded of
   ongoing confidentiality obligations at exit.

## Enforcement
Failure to complete required onboarding/offboarding steps is a policy violation and should be
tracked to remediation.
