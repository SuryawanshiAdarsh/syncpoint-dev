# Risk Assessment Policy

> **Starting template.** Customize this document to reflect your organization's actual
> practices before adopting it as policy. This is not legal advice.

## Purpose
Establishes a repeatable process for identifying, assessing, prioritizing, and treating risks
to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company and customer information.

## Scope
Applies to all systems, vendors, processes, and personnel that could introduce risk to the
organization's information assets.

## Policy

1. **Risk identification.** Risks are identified from multiple sources: system architecture
   reviews, vendor assessments, audit findings, incidents, and industry threat intelligence.
2. **Risk assessment.** Each identified risk is assessed for likelihood and impact and
   assigned a risk rating (e.g. Low/Medium/High/Critical).
3. **Risk register.** Risks are tracked in a risk register with owner, rating, treatment plan,
   and target resolution date.
4. **Risk treatment.** For each material risk, the organization decides to accept, mitigate,
   transfer, or avoid the risk, and documents the rationale.
5. **Periodic assessment.** A formal risk assessment is performed at least annually and after
   any material change to the business, systems, or threat landscape.
6. **Reporting.** High and Critical risks are reported to leadership with a remediation
   timeline.

## Enforcement
Failure to track and remediate identified Critical risks within an agreed timeline should be
escalated to leadership.
